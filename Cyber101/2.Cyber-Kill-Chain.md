# Cyber Kill Chain - Learning Notes

## 1. Reconnaissance
The attacker collects publicly available information about the target organization, such as employee details, email addresses, internet-facing services, and business-related information.

## 2. Weaponization
The attacker prepares malicious content or tools that may be used during the operation, including phishing materials or malware payloads.

## 3. Delivery
The malicious content is delivered to the target through channels such as email attachments, web links, or other communication methods.

## 4. Exploitation
A vulnerability or user action is leveraged to gain initial access to systems or accounts.

## 5. Installation
The attacker establishes persistence or deploys components that allow continued access to the compromised environment.

## 6. Command and Control (C2)
Communication is established between compromised systems and external infrastructure to maintain remote access and coordinate activities.

## 7. Actions on Objectives
The attacker attempts to achieve operational goals, such as data access, business disruption, information theft, or other targeted objectives.

## Key Learning
- Understand the attack lifecycle.
- Recognize attacker behaviors across different stages.
- Improve threat detection and incident response awareness.
- Apply Cyber Kill Chain concepts to security monitoring and analysis.
